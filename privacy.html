Privacy policy for Facade Crew

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

Facade Crew ("the game") is made by Facade Crew, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. The only way to contact us is by email: omer@silome.xyz.

The short version

  • There is no account and no login. We never ask for your name, email or phone number inside the game.
  • Your progress (jobs, stars, coins, hats and settings) is saved only on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and uses Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config to see how the game is played and to adjust it. These Google services collect some device information, described below.
  • In the UK and the European Economic Area you're asked for consent first. You can change your choice at any time in the game, under Settings, then Privacy choices.
  • We do not sell your data.

Information stored only on your phone

Game progress, coins, settings and your daily reward streak are stored on your phone. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example, "your daily reward is ready") are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The game shows optional rewarded ads (for example, to continue after a collapse or to double your coins) and occasional full-screen ads between rounds. They come from Google AdMob, which collects:

How you interact with the game and with ads (launches, taps, video views)Advertising and analytics

Google processes this data under Google's Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your consent and ad choices

  • Consent message (UK and EEA): the first time you open the game, Google's consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related uses of your data. If you don't agree, you may still see ads, but they are non-personalised: they are based on things like the game you're playing and your general location, not on a profile of you.
  • Changing your mind: open Settings, then Privacy choices in the game at any time to change or withdraw your consent.
  • Your advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (usually Settings, then Google, then Ads, or Settings, then Privacy, then Ads).
  • Buying "No Ads" turns off the ads between rounds. Rewarded ads stay available, and only play if you tap one.

Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, for example which jobs are too hard, where players stop playing, and how often ads are watched, so we can make the game better. It collects:

  • An app-instance ID (a random number for this install of the game, not your name).
  • Game events, such as starting, finishing or failing a job, buying something in the coin shop, and watching an ad.
  • Device and app information, such as phone model, operating system version, language, and app version.
  • General location (country or region), worked out from your IP address. Google does not keep the IP address itself in Analytics.

Analytics only stores data about you if your consent choices allow it. If you're in the UK or EEA and haven't agreed to the uses in the consent message, analytics storage stays off. You can change this at any time in Settings, then Privacy choices. Data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information.

Game settings from the internet: Firebase Remote Config

Firebase Remote Config lets us adjust some game settings without an update, for example how often ads appear between rounds and how fast the difficulty rises. To fetch these settings, the game uses a Firebase installation ID (a random ID for this install) and basic device information such as the app version and language. If the game can't reach the internet, it uses its built-in settings.

Crash reporting

The game does not currently use a separate crash-reporting service (such as Firebase Crashlytics). If we add one, we'll update this policy first.

Purchases: Google Play Billing

If you buy "No Ads", the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google tells the game that the purchase was made, so it can switch the ads between rounds off, and so it can restore the purchase if you reinstall the game or move to a new phone.

The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to Google Play. When a purchase is made or restored, Unity receives purchase and transaction information (product, price, transaction details), installation and session identifiers, device information (model, operating system version, language) and your country worked out from your IP address. Unity uses this to run the purchase service, for example to avoid counting the same purchase twice. Unity keeps its service logs for 30 days. See Unity's privacy policy.

The game engine

The game is made with the Unity game engine. Unity Analytics and Unity's cloud crash reporting are switched off in the game's settings. The only Unity online service the game uses is In-App Purchasing, described above.

Children

The game is made for adults, and its audience is adults aged 18 to 44. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has used the game and you want their data deleted, email us.

How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the game.
  • Firebase Analytics: event data is kept for 2 months, the shortest period Google Analytics offers, and then deleted.
  • AdMob, Remote Config and Unity: kept by Google and Unity under their own retention rules, linked above.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as we need them to help you, then deleted.

Why we're allowed to use this data

Personalised ads and analytics rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and Remote Config rely on our legitimate interest in running a free, ad-supported game safely. Handling a purchase is needed to give you what you paid for.

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how it's used, and to withdraw consent. We don't hold data that names you, so the quickest way to manage ad and analytics data is through Settings, then Privacy choices in the game and the advertising ID settings on your phone. For any request, including deleting analytics data linked to your install, email omer@silome.xyz and we'll help. We reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
