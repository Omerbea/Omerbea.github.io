Facade Crew is a cozy one-tap building game for Android, made by Facade Crew.

Email: omer@silome.xyz

Please tell us your phone model and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

I bought "No Ads" and the ads came back

Open the game, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to Google Play with the same Google account you used to buy it. The purchase is also restored automatically when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone with the same account. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of the purchase (you can find it in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions, then Budget and history).

Why do I see ads, and what does "No Ads" remove?

The game is free because of ads. "No Ads" is a one-off purchase that removes the ads between rounds for good. Rewarded ads (to continue after a collapse or to double your coins) stay available, and they only play when you tap one.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

In the game, open Settings and tap Privacy choices. You'll see Google's consent message again and can change your answer. You can also reset your advertising ID, or turn off ads personalisation, in your phone's settings. Our privacy policy explains what is collected and why.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Your game progress is stored only on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your install, email omer@silome.xyz with the subject "Data deletion". We'll confirm when it's done, within one month.

My progress has gone

Progress is saved on your phone only, so it doesn't move to a new phone, and it's lost if the game is uninstalled or its storage is cleared. A "No Ads" purchase is the exception: it's restored from your Google Play account.
