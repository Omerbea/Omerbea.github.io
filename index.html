Build it up. Fix it up. Don't let it wobble!

Façade Crew is a cozy one-tap building game. Scaffold the front of each building to the roof, fix the town on the way up, and keep it all from coming down.

Coming soon to Google Play

Free to play. Android first, iPhone later.

One tap at a time. Put up the poles, then drop each board level as it slides or swings past.
Fix the town. Leaks, broken windows, peeling paint and the odd stuck cat.
30 jobs. From a little cottage to a 30-floor skyscraper, with 3 stars to earn on each.
Gets harder as you get better. Rank up from Rookie to Master crew.
